# Fisk visual direction

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — A market question enters an evidence observatory, separates into independently inspectable bands, and returns as a conditional thesis whose uncertainty remains visible.

**OWN-WORLD** — Warm mineral paper, charcoal ink, dense graphite, and one spectral violet instrument colour. Editorial Manrope and measurement-only JetBrains Mono connect the public journal to the research desk.

**STORY** — Ask a real question; watch sources gather; inspect the market pulse; read the newsroom; enter a desk shaped around the evidence; retain the human decision.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Brand, session state, monumental product promise, working research question, and a circular aperture that shows evidence converging into a conditional result.

**FORM** — Responsive editorial web application. The committed direction is “The evidence observatory”; the recorded Impeccable surface seed is `2d8f1112`.

## The evidence observatory

Fisk treats a market question like a signal entering an observatory: it separates into evidence bands—price, news, filings, tokenized activity, and macro context—before being recombined as a conditional thesis.

The public surface is an editorial research journal in mineral paper, charcoal ink, and one spectral violet accent. The signature first viewport is a working evidence aperture rather than a dashboard screenshot. The research desk reverses the material into graphite while retaining the same spectral band, compact measurements, typography, and precise circular instrument details.

The landing page must make three things clear within its first viewport: Fisk is market research, the interface gathers several kinds of evidence, and the user can ask a real question immediately. Motion reveals the evidence spectrum once and then settles.

### System commitments

- Warm mineral canvas and dense graphite desk, connected by spectral violet.
- Manrope for authored editorial language; JetBrains Mono only for measurements, symbols, timestamps, and source states.
- Circular apertures for observation and status; 12–15px corners for functional modules; pills only for compact controls.
- Varied editorial composition with one dominant story instead of uniform cards.
- Data states always show both text and colour.
- The mobile desk becomes one prioritized column with the composer fixed as a bottom instrument.

### Source raises from the direction roll

- Nixie counter: numerical changes must feel precise and physically placed.
- HyperCard: every evidence object should remain directly addressable.
- Labanotation: research progress should read as an ordered sequence.
- Linocut broadside: the main claim deserves monumental scale and crop discipline.
- Daylight section: time and freshness should be structural, not metadata afterthoughts.
- Gravity garden: evidence bands should visibly converge without turning into decoration.
