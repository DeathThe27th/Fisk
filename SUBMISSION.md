# Fisk — hackathon submission notes

## Demo path

Ask: **How exposed is NVDA to this weekend's semiconductor news, what is the market pricing in, and what would invalidate the bullish thesis?**

The demo shows Fisk classify the question, retrieve Bitget RWA candles, normalize Finnhub news, attach recent SEC filings, synthesize a cited answer with Qwen, stress-test the thesis, state unknowns, and preserve the human decision boundary.

## Qwen’s role

Qwen receives normalized evidence without provider secrets or privileged objects. It creates the direct answer, conditional thesis, evidence-linked findings, catalysts, bull and bear cases, stress tests, invalidation conditions, unknowns, and module selection. One compact review instruction checks unsupported claims, stale evidence, contradictions, and missing counterarguments.

## Submission checklist

- Public demo URL and repository
- Full project description and supporting screenshots or video
- Exact Qwen role described above
- X post containing `#BitgetHackathon` and `@Bitget_AI`
- Full university name if entering the University Special Prize
